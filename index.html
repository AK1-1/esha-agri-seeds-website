About Us

Pioneering Seeds for India's Farmers

Esha Agri Seeds Pvt. Ltd., an ISO 9001 & 2008 certified company established in 2009 with its head office at Hyderabad, India, was founded with the vision of creating better livelihoods for Indian farmers.

Our core strength lies in vegetable hybrid seeds breeding, production and marketing — supported by professionally qualified and experienced personnel. We acknowledge services of AVRDC, Taiwan, and IIHR, Bangalore in our research program.
